Privacy Policy — PAPA Voronka

Last updated: 15 October 2026

PAPA Voronka ("the app") is operated by Ozod Jamol, the owner of the Instagram account @paparazzi.uz and the Telegram channels PaparazziUZ and Xo'jaobod City 24. The app is used only for these accounts, which are owned and managed by the operator.

What the app does

When someone comments on a post of @paparazzi.uz, the app may send that person one private reply in Instagram Direct with links to our Telegram channels. When someone requests to join our Telegram channel, our bot sends a welcome message and approves the request.

Data we process

  • Instagram: comment ID, comment text, commenter's Instagram-scoped user ID and username, post ID, time.
  • Telegram: user ID of a person who requested to join, the invite link used, time.

We do not collect phone numbers, e-mail addresses, passwords, private conversations, or payment data. We do not sell, rent or share data with third parties and do not use it for advertising profiles.

Why

Only to send the requested link once per person per post, avoid duplicate messages, and count how many people joined our channels from each post (aggregated statistics).

Storage and retention

Data is stored on our own server (DigitalOcean, EU) with restricted access, and is deleted automatically after 90 days, or earlier on request. Aggregated counts without personal identifiers may be kept longer.

Your rights and data deletion

You can ask us to delete all data related to you at any time — see Data deletion instructions. You can also block messages from @paparazzi.uz in Instagram or leave the Telegram channel at any time.
